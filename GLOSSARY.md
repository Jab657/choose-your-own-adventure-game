# Choose Your Own Adventure

A text adventure where a player creates a character, picks a path, and faces encounters such as the dragon and the merchant.

## Language

**Merchant**:
A trader met once per game on the South path who sells one item: Magical Armor or a Magical Weapon.
_Avoid_: Shop, vendor, trader

**Magical Armor**:
Armor that absorbs a fixed amount of damage from every hit that gets past the player's defense.
_Avoid_: Shield, protection, defense

**Magical Weapon**:
The player's occupation weapon after enchantment, with a higher maximum damage. It is the same weapon, not a replacement.
_Avoid_: New weapon, upgrade

**Detour**:
A short story scene the player always meets on the North path before reaching the dragon. It has its own description and one choice, and the player always carries on to the dragon afterwards.
_Avoid_: Encounter, event, side path, side quest

**Shrine**:
The Detour on the North path: an old mountain shrine where the player can make an offering of Gold, pray, or walk past. Offerings and prayers change the player's health.
_Avoid_: Altar, temple

**Gold**:
The player's currency: a fixed amount every character starts with, spent at the Merchant or as an offering at the Shrine.
_Avoid_: Money, coins, currency
